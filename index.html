🇦🇺 Australian CV Rules 澳洲求職履歷必守原則
  • Strict 1 Page:嚴格一頁 A4： Hospitality & entry roles review in 10 seconds. Keep concise.澳洲餐飲與初階職缺主管僅看 10 秒，一頁為黃金標準。
  • No Personal Photo:絕對不放照片： Anti-discrimination laws. Never include age, gender, marital status or DOB.澳洲就業反歧視法規定：不可放照片、年齡、性別、婚姻或生日。
  • Clear Visa Details:寫明簽證與到期日： State WHV 417/462 and valid expiry so employers know you have legal rights.明確標示簽證類別（417/462）與到期年月，確認合法全職工作權。
  • Roster & Start Date:強調可立即上工與班表彈性： Immediate start, weekends, early mornings and evenings are major advantages.清晨開班、週末、國定假日無限制是澳洲雇主最重視的優勢。
  • Transferable Skills:跨領域強調共通技能： Highlight speed, cash handling, customer care, multitasking & physical stamina.突顯收銀找零、外語溝通、多工處理、團隊合作與體力耐受度。
  • Local Tickets:必備澳洲證照： WA RSA (liquor), Food Handler (DoFoodSafely), White Card (construction).酒類服務必備 RSA、食品從業 DoFoodSafely、工地建築需白卡。
